Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how SpotTribe Bali uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

We utilize essential cookies for user authentication (such as Google OAuth login sessions for posting reviews) and analytics cookies to understand visitor navigation patterns. Additionally, third-party vendors like Google AdSense use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

3. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off cookies through your individual browser settings. For more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, check the browsers' respective websites.
